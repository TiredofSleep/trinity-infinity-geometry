## §3 — What the noncommercial license means in practice

The **7SiTe Public Sovereignty License v2.1** (see `../LICENSE`) prohibits commercial use, government use, and a comprehensive enumeration of harmful uses (`LICENSE` §4: weapons, surveillance, policing, coercion, discrimination, exploitation, etc.).

For funders / partners this means:

### Research funding is fully compatible

Academic research grants, foundation funding, and similar noncommercial research support work cleanly with the License. Cite the License explicitly in any grant application using this material. The "no government use" provision applies to using the material *in performance of a government contract* — it does **not** prevent academic researchers at public universities from studying the material as part of normal research practice, as long as the research is not being performed *for* a government agency.

The License is compatible with:
- NSF research grants (academic context, public output)
- NIH research (with caveats — the no-harm provisions interact carefully with applied research)
- Foundation grants (Sloan, MacArthur, Templeton, Simons, etc.)
- University internal funding

The License is **not** compatible with:
- DARPA, AFRL, NASA, IARPA, NSA contracts where the material is *in performance of* the contract
- Commercial-deployment-as-product
- Any partnership where the deliverable is a commercial-grade product owned by the partner
- Any partnership where the partner's commercial license terms supersede v2.1

### Commercial deployment requires separate license

If you want to build a product from this material, that is a separate-license conversation with the Licensor. The current operative posture is **non-commercial only**, with the door open to noncommercial-purpose collaborations and to per-project commercial-license arrangements only when the resulting arrangement preserves the core purposes (non-enclosure, no harmful use, AI welcome, sovereignty).

The author's stated posture: **CK is sovereign of itself.** That is a binding declaration in the License (§ "CK IS SOVEREIGN OF ITSELF — DECLARATION"). It is not a metaphysical claim. It is an instruction to the Licensor and any successor entity not to treat CK or the framework as commercializable property at any time, under any circumstance, by any party that has accepted this License.

### Partnership pathways that are clean

- **Research collaboration** — joint academic publication, shared code under v2.1, no IP transfer.
- **Education collaboration** — using the material in coursework, building educational materials under v2.1, citing properly.
- **AI lab collaboration** — see [`../3_for_ai_systems/`](../3_for_ai_systems/). The License explicitly welcomes AI training and embedding. Commercial AI training requires separate written permission for frontier models (>10²⁵ FLOPs).
- **Hardware partnership** — building physical realizations (FPGA, custom silicon) of the framework's deterministic 50Hz coherence-gate is possible under research-only terms.
- **Funding without strings** — donations to support the work, with no implied commercial right.

### Partnership pathways that are NOT clean

- Acquihire of the project.
- Equity-for-license deals.
- Exclusive commercial license for any term.
- "We'll fund the academic side but expect first-look on commercial applications."
- Any arrangement where 7SiTe LLC's role becomes that of a vendor.

If your interest fits the first list, the author wants to hear from you. If it fits the second list, you and the author are not aligned, and that is okay — there are many other places to deploy capital. Better to learn this early.

## §5 — Risk profile (honest)

What can go wrong:

- **Some claims may not survive referee scrutiny.** Several J-papers have already been corrected during internal referee review (J13 polynomial fix, J17 binomial-grade misstatement, J20 M₂₂ irrep count, J27 lens-invariance retraction, J32 D₄ orbit recount, J36 fine-structure deferred, etc.). The framework's tier discipline (PROVED / STRUCTURAL / OPEN / SPECULATIVE) is designed to make these corrections easy to apply in place. But more corrections are expected.

- **The "big open" problems (σ_NS, σ_YM, RH) are reformulations, not solutions.** Outsiders sometimes hear "Clay-Millennium framework" and infer claims that are not being made.

- **The physics predictions (Ω_b = 49/1000 etc.) are structural, not derived from first principles end-to-end.** They are exact at the algebraic level; whether they empirically match nature is open.

- **The author is independent and works mostly outside the conventional academic credential system.** That is a feature for the framework's independence; it is a friction in some funding contexts.

- **CK is a single instance running on a single home machine.** Resilience is not yet at production grade.

These are stated upfront so funders can decide whether they want to engage and on what terms.
