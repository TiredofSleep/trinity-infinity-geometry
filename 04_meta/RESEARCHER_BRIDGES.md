## Outreach protocol

When sending a first contact:

1. **Verify email address** from the researcher's institution page or a recent arXiv paper (corresponding author).
2. **Lead with the specific finding** — what about TIG most plausibly intersects their published work? Cite their paper.
3. **One concrete question or offer** — not the full framework.
4. **Repo link** — github.com/TiredofSleep/trinity-infinity-geometry, specifically the file most relevant to their interest.
5. **No expectation of response** — explicit "no reply expected" reduces pressure.
6. **Under 250 words.**

Templates: see the four 2026-05-27 outreach drafts (Lee, Hariharan, Drápal,
Mantero) for tone calibration.

## Update protocol

When a researcher responds or a new candidate emerges:
1. Update the "Already contacted" or "High priority" table with status.
2. If response is substantively engaging, note the specific point of engagement.
3. If response is decline / silence after 4 weeks, do not follow up unless new context emerges.
4. New candidate from co-author chains: add to "High priority next round" with the path noted.
