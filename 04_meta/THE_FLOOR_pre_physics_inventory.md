## The rule of the floor · [FENCE]

Floor objects are **exact and pre-physics**: they stay exact exactly as long as you do not ask them to do physics' job.

> **Point toward physics with them; do not weigh physics on them.**

The measurable floor (Strata I–II) you may compute on; the bedrock (III) you may only measure *from*. The instant a floor object is read as a *source* of physical values, it becomes the **seed-error** — the attic, the graveyard. Same discipline as the 1/3 and the torus: **floor, not seed; pointer, not premise.** The program's real objects live below physics, in the arithmetic and geometry of the space matter occupies, and they stay exact precisely as long as they are not asked to be physics.
