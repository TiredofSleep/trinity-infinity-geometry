Trinity Infinity Geometry

A new way to teach higher mathematics, by classifying its paradoxes rather than resolving them, and the research results it keeps producing. Every shape and every floor is a coin: two sides and an edge.

Theory of Nothing is the closest there will ever be to Theory of Everything.
— Brayden Sanders, March 2026

Results

  • Quantum information · finite fieldsThe magic of a cubic-phase qudit state is the L1 norm of a Walsh spectrum. So no magic state of a third-level diagonal gate reaches the Wigner-negativity ceiling. On three qudits of 3, 5 and 7 levels the norm state of the field with p³ elements is the most magic of them; from 11 on, elliptic-curve states beat it. Seven levels
  • Number theory · magic statesThe norm state's Wigner values are Kloosterman sums, on three qudits of every odd prime, so its negativity is known in closed form and tends to 8/(3π) of the ceiling. The proof
  • Quantum chaos · magic statesThe oscillator states' magic follows the Sato–Tate law, and averaged over a torus's eigenbasis the fourth moment of their Wigner values is exactly 2p/(p + 1). The theorem
  • Quantum contextualitySmall genuine magic sets for every odd number of qubits: 16 observables at five qubits against a published 26, and 16 is the minimum. The theorem
  • Linear algebraThe determinant of MATLAB's odd magic squares, in closed form; every pandiagonal 5×5 magic square is invertible. The void lens

How the work stays honest

Each direction starts from the author's intuition, made exact. Predictions are registered in a public commit before anything runs, a second independent check confirms each result, a literature gate credits what is known, and every failed prediction is kept in the graveyard with the evidence that killed it. The computations are AI-assisted and machine-checked; the directions are the author's. How we work.
