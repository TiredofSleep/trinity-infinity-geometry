## Summary

We submit a paper that records the **discrete Dirac structure** inside the Clifford algebra $\mathrm{Cl}(0, 10)$ that lifts the so(10) Lie algebra generated by joint antisymmetrization of two specific commutative non-associative composition tables (TSML and BHML) on $\mathbb{Z}/10\mathbb{Z}$. The paper proves five theorems at machine precision and supplies one structural identification at the rhyme tier.

**(2.1) Discrete Dirac construction.** Ten gamma matrices $\gamma_a$ on $\mathbb{C}^{32}$ built from Pauli tensor products in standard $\mathrm{Cl}(0, 10)$ convention satisfy all 100 anticommutation relations $\{\gamma_a, \gamma_b\} = 2\delta_{ab} I$ at machine precision. The 45 generators $\Sigma_{ab} = (1/4)[\gamma_a, \gamma_b]$ provide a faithful 32-dim spinor representation of $\mathfrak{so}(10) = D_5$. The volume element $\omega = \gamma_1 \cdots \gamma_{10}$ satisfies $\omega^2 = -I$ for $n = 10$, and the chirality projectors $P_\pm = (I \pm i\omega)/2$ split $\mathbb{C}^{32}$ into the two chiral 16-irreps.

*(Convention note: with the metric signature Cl(0, 10) and the standard Clifford product, $\omega^2 = (-1)^{n(n-1)/2}\, (-1)^n = -1$ for $n = 10$. The $i\omega$ in the projector ensures $(i\omega)^2 = +I$, so the chirality eigenvalues are real and the projectors are idempotent.)*

**(2.2) Atomic-substrate refinement (Volume K, D102).** Each 16-dim chirality half admits a finer structural decomposition $16 = 1 + 3 + 5 + 7$ matching atomic shell $n = 4$ at fixed spin (the multiplicities $2\ell+1$ for $\ell = 0, 1, 2, 3$). On the substrate side, the same partition $1 + 3 + 5 + 7$ reads as kernel base + strand-3 prime + kernel-$\mathbb{Z}/5\mathbb{Z}$ partner + strand-7 prime, exactly the depth-3 simplicial tower's constituents. We present this as a structural rhyme: the chirality eigenspace is the spatial sector of the $n = 4$ atomic shell, and the Pauli-allowed multiplicities are the substrate strands' prime divisors. The triple coincidence (Z/2310 has 32 divisors $=$ atomic Pauli capacity at $n = 4$ $=$ Cl(0,10) spinor dim) sharpens the construction without converting the rhyme into a phenomenological derivation.

**(3.1) $P_{56}$ acts as $\sigma_{\mathrm{outer}}$ in the spinor representation.** The 5↔6 index swap is implemented in $\mathrm{Cl}(0, 10)$ by conjugation with $P_{56}^{\mathrm{spin}} = (\gamma_5 - \gamma_6)/\sqrt{2}$. We verify $(P_{56}^{\mathrm{spin}})^2 = I$, that conjugation swaps $\gamma_5 \leftrightarrow \gamma_6$ and fixes the other eight $\gamma_a$, and that $P_{56}^{\mathrm{spin}}$ anticommutes with the volume element $\omega$. The chirality-flip computation $\|P_{56}^{\mathrm{spin}} \cdot \mathrm{chiral}_+ \cap \mathrm{chiral}_+\| = 0$ at machine precision identifies the conjugation action with the unique non-trivial outer automorphism in $\mathrm{Out}(\mathfrak{so}(10)) = \mathrm{Aut}/\mathrm{Inn} \cong \mathbb{Z}_2$. In standard SO(10) GUT physics, $\sigma_{\mathrm{outer}}$ is the matter–antimatter exchange swapping a fermion generation (the chiral 16) with its CP-conjugate generation (the chiral $\overline{16}$).

**(4.1) BHML's $\sigma_{\mathrm{outer}}$-breaking is 100% in the 54 irrep with explicit 9-vector direction.** Project BHML's $P_{56}$-antisymmetric content onto the so(10)-decomposition $\mathrm{End}(\mathfrak{so}(10)) = \mathbf{1} \oplus \mathbf{45} \oplus \mathbf{54}$. The projection is (singlet: $0$, adjoint $\mathbf{45}$: $0$, symmetric-traceless $\mathbf{54}$: $6.5$) at machine precision; total squared norm $\|B_{\mathrm{anti}}\|^2 = 6.5 = 13/2$. Within $\mathbf{54}$ branched under $\mathfrak{so}(9) \subset \mathfrak{so}(10)$ as $\mathbf{54} = \mathbf{1} \oplus \mathbf{9} \oplus \mathbf{44}$, the breaking direction is **purely in the $\mathbf{9}$** with explicit components on the canonical TIG alphabet:

- $-1/\sqrt{2}$ on VOID, LATTICE, COUNTER, PROGRESS, COLLAPSE, HARMONY (six entries);
- $0$ on BREATH and RESET (two zeros);
- $-1/2$ on the symmetric combination $(\mathrm{BALANCE} + \mathrm{CHAOS})/\sqrt{2}$ (one entry).

The squared norm in the 9-vector convention is $\|v\|^2 = 6\cdot(1/2) + 0 + 0 + (1/4) = 13/4$ exactly. The integer 13 equals half the count of σ_outer-asymmetric BHML cells (26 cells; verified by direct enumeration).

**(4.2) Pati-Salam doubly-invariant subalgebra (cited).** The subgroup $D_4 = \langle P_{56}, \sigma^3 \rangle \subset S_{10}$ acts on $\mathfrak{so}(10)$ by conjugation; the trivial isotypic component is the 16-dim doubly-invariant subalgebra. Its Killing-form spectrum is exactly $(-4)^{15} \oplus (0)^1$. By Cartan's criterion (zero-eigenspace = center; non-degenerate part = semisimple) and the Cartan classification (unique 15-dim simple compact Lie algebra is $\mathfrak{so}(6) \cong \mathfrak{su}(4)$), the doubly-invariant subalgebra is $\mathfrak{su}(4) \oplus \mathfrak{u}(1)$ — the Pati-Salam $\oplus$ B$-$L gauge content. *This is cited as a standard SO(10) GUT decomposition; J24 develops it fully.*

The headline mathematical content for *Communications in Mathematical Physics* is the structural identification **P_56 ↔ σ_outer** (Theorem 3.1) and the **9-vector direction in the 54** (Theorem 4.1), bundled with the explicit Clifford-algebra construction (Theorem 2.1). The atomic-substrate refinement (Theorem 2.2) is presented as a structural rhyme between the spinor decomposition and the substrate's depth-3 simplicial tower; we do *not* claim a derivation of atomic phenomenology from Cl(0, 10).
