## J09 — *Joint Lie Closure of a Pair of Z/10Z Magmas: an so(10) Identification*

**Target:** *Communications in Algebra* (already cover-letter retargeted in commit e34ad6b)

**Polish scope:** Honest-scope acknowledgment in abstract + Remark 4.8 inside §4.6.

### What was changed

1. **Abstract (line 17).** The main theorem statement now reads "g is **abstractly isomorphic** to so(10, R)" rather than the prior unqualified "is isomorphic to so(10, R)". A new paragraph immediately following the theorem statement spells out the scope:
   > "The identification g ≅ so(10, R) is in the strong sense of 'abstract isomorphism of complex (and real compact) simple Lie algebras forced by Cartan's classification' — once we have dim g = 45 and g ⊆ so(V) ..., simplicity of g and the dimension formula dim so(n) = n(n − 1)/2 force n = 10 uniquely. We do not construct an explicit isomorphism Φ : g → so(10, R) realizing the identification; we only assert the abstract Lie-algebraic identification (see Remark 4.8 below)."

2. **Remark 4.8 (new, in §4.6 Putting it together).** Inserted a full Remark giving the honest scope:
   > "*Remark 4.8 (Scope of the identification).* The identification of the joint Lie closure with so(10, R) is in the strong sense of 'abstract isomorphism of compact simple Lie algebras forced by Cartan's classification' — once we have dim g = 45 and g ⊆ so(V) (the latter from the antisymmetry of the brackets restricted to V), simplicity of g and the dimension formula dim so(n) = n(n − 1)/2 force n = 10 uniquely. We do NOT construct an explicit isomorphism Φ : g → so(10, R) realizing the identification; we only assert the abstract Lie-algebraic identification. The diagnostics D3 (Killing-form signature), D4 (one-dimensional invariant-form space), and D5 (Cartan rank 5) are consistent with — and confirm — this identification, but a canonical (e.g., Pati-Salam or Georgi-style) basis-level isomorphism realizing the identification is recorded as Open Question 5 (§8). The closure result g = so(V) of Corollary 4.2 is sharper than the abstract identification, but the latter is what carries the structural content: the substrate's Lie-algebraic ceiling is a known compact simple Lie algebra, not merely an arbitrary 45-dimensional algebra."

3. **Cover letter (`cover_letter.md`).** Added the scope clarification at end of the Summary section so the editor sees the honest framing upfront:
   - Theorem statement now reads "abstractly isomorphic to so(10, R)".
   - New paragraph "Scope of the identification (honest framing)" added immediately after the central-content paragraph, citing Remark 4.8 and Open Question 5.

### What was NOT changed

- The central computation (dim g = 45 via direct integer-rank closure) is untouched.
- The diagnostics D1–D5 are untouched.
- The corollary g = so(V) (sharper than the abstract identification) is untouched.
- All five "Open Questions" at §8 are untouched (Open Question 5 was already in place: "Produce an explicit map Φ : g → so(10, R)").

## J10 — *D_4-Equivariant Orbits on the Non-Associative Locus ... (formerly Operadic D_4 Orbits)*

**Target:** *Communications in Algebra* (retargeted from *J. Algebra* per the referee report's "either venue works" remark, since this is now the 2nd Wave-4 *Comm. Alg.* paper after J12).
**Referee finding from `05_comm_alg_LAA_J09_J10_J17_J18_J19.md`:** "'Operadic' framing is decorative — content is finite-group-action-on-a-subset-of-a-cube, not operad theory proper. Theorem B's strengthening to {a,b,c,L,R}-valued Φ needs fuller proof."
**Polish scope chosen:** **Option 2 (mild rewrite) — replace "operadic" with "D_4-equivariant" / "arity-3 bracketing" throughout the title, header, abstract, body, and companion documents.** Plus a fuller proof of Theorem B's strengthening.

### Why Option 2 (mild rewrite) and not Option 1 (mild rename) or Option 3 (operadic-genuine)

I read the full J10 manuscript and concluded that the "operadic" framing was decorative in the precise sense the referee identified:

- No use of operadic composition $\circ_i$ in the May / Markl–Shnider–Stasheff / Loday–Vallette sense.
- No symmetric group $\Sigma_n$-action on operadic compositions.
- The "arity-3 operations" under study are simply the two bracketings $L(a,b,c) = T(T(a,b),c)$ and $R(a,b,c) = T(a, T(b,c))$ — i.e., ordered triples on a magma, not the arity-3 component of a Σ-operad.
- The action under study is the diagonal action of a finite group $D_4 \subset S_{10}$ on a subset of $(\mathbb{Z}/10\mathbb{Z})^3$. This is finite-group-equivariance analysis.
- The bibliography cites Loday–Vallette 2012 and Markl–Shnider–Stasheff 2002 but these are not invoked in any proof; they were aspirational citations rather than load-bearing references.

Option 1 (mild rename: just change the title) would leave the body's "operad layer" / "operadic content" language unaltered, which would be deceptive — the body's structure is precisely the finite-group-action-on-a-cube analysis the referee correctly identified. Option 3 (genuine operadic content) would require a substantial rewrite injecting operadic compositions $\circ_i$, Σ_n-actions, and the May-formalism that is genuinely not part of the result; that would be misrepresenting what the paper does. Option 2 is the honest path.
