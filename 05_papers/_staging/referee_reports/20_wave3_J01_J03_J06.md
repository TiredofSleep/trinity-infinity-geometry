**Key wording (new Remark immediately following Theorem 4):**
> "**Remark on the X_0(p) vs X_0(p)^+ distinction.** Ogg's statement involves the Atkin–Lehner quotient X_0(p)^+ = X_0(p)/⟨w_p⟩, not X_0(p) plain. For the 15 supersingular primes both curves do have genus 0, but for some larger primes (e.g., 37, 43, 53, 61, 67, 73, 79, 83, 89, 101, 113, 131) the distinction is nontrivial: the precise statement is 'p supersingular ⟺ X_0(p)^+ has genus 0,' equivalently '⟺ the normalizer N(Γ_0(p)) in PSL_2(R) admits a Hauptmodul.'"
