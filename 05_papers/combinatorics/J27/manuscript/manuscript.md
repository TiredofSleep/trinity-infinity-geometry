## Abstract

The set C = {1, 3, 7, 9} of multiplicative units of the ring Z/10Z is a sub-magma of the canonical TSML composition lattice CL_TSML in the sense that CL_TSML[i, j] ∈ {3, 7} ⊆ C for all i, j ∈ C, where CL_TSML is the 73-HARMONY composition table on Z/10Z (Sanders–Gish, J33). We prove this closure directly on the 16 cells of C × C and contrast it with the *joint 4-core* {0, 7, 8, 9} studied in the companion four-core paper, the unique 4-element subset of Z/10Z jointly closed under both CL_TSML and CL_BHML.

The two 4-element sub-magmas have distinct closure properties:

1. C is the multiplicative-unit group (Z/10Z)* (a cyclic group of order 4, generated by 3); it is one of *78* four-element subsets of Z/10Z that are TSML-closed.
2. The joint 4-core {0, 7, 8, 9} is the *unique* four-element subset of Z/10Z that is closed under both CL_TSML and CL_BHML simultaneously.
3. C is *not* closed under CL_BHML: CL_BHML[1, 1] = 2 ∉ C, and in fact none of the 16 cells of CL_BHML ∣_{C × C} lies in C (the image is {0, 2, 4, 6, 8}, disjoint from C).

The contrast between (1) and (2) gives the structural distinction expressed by the title: the multiplicative-unit subgroup C is forced by the ring structure of Z/10Z alone and lives in the TSML lattice as one TSML-closed sub-magma among many; the joint 4-core is forced by simultaneous TSML/BHML closure and is unique at its size. The two answer different structural questions about the substrate.

In §5 we record a generator-selection result (D19): among the two primitive roots of (Z/10Z)* ≅ Z/4Z, namely g = 3 and g = 7, only g = 3 is compatible with the TIG flatness ratio T* = BALANCE/HARMONY = 5/g³ ∈ (0, 1). Under g = 7, HARMONY = 7³ ≡ 3 (mod 10), yielding T* = 5/3 > 1.

## Lens and substrate

This paper works on the finite ring Z/10Z with two specific 10 × 10 multiplication tables, CL_TSML and CL_BHML, as defined in the companion forcing paper J33. These tables are not derived from first principles; they reflect a structural reading of Z/10Z motivated by the 10-operator decomposition of the residues and by observed dynamical behavior. The theorems below are theorems on this specific substrate and on these specific tables. Whether analogous tables on Z/N Z (N ≠ 10) or on F_p give similarly rich downstream connections is open.

## §1 Introduction

The ring Z/10Z has the multiplicative units (Z/10Z)* = {n ∈ Z/10Z : gcd(n, 10) = 1} = {1, 3, 7, 9}. Under the canonical TSML composition lattice CL_TSML on Z/10Z (J33), C = (Z/10Z)* is closed: for all i, j ∈ C, CL_TSML[i, j] ∈ C. The image of C × C under CL_TSML is the two-element subset {3, 7} ⊆ C.

This paper establishes the closure and contextualizes it within the sub-magma inventory of Z/10Z under the canonical operators CL_TSML and CL_BHML.

### §1.1 Scope statement

This paper makes *no claim* of lens-invariance for C. An earlier draft asserted (incorrectly) that C was closed under both CL_TSML and CL_BHML. Computational verification shows the contrary: CL_BHML[1, 1] = 2 ∉ C, and in fact none of the 16 cells of CL_BHML ∣_{C × C} lies in C (Proposition 4.1). We are explicit: C is TSML-closed only. The unique 4-element subset of Z/10Z closed under both CL_TSML and CL_BHML is the joint 4-core {0, 7, 8, 9}; we contrast the two cases in §4.

### §1.2 What is closure?

For a 10 × 10 multiplication table M : Z/10Z × Z/10Z → Z/10Z and a subset S ⊆ Z/10Z, S is *M-closed* (or a *sub-magma* of M) if M[i, j] ∈ S for all i, j ∈ S. S is *jointly M, M'-closed* if it is M-closed and M'-closed simultaneously.

## §2 The corner C and its multiplicative structure

**Definition 2.1.** The *corner* of Z/10Z is the set C = {1, 3, 7, 9} of residues coprime to 10, equivalently the multiplicative-unit group (Z/10Z)*.

**Lemma 2.2.** Under ordinary multiplication mod 10, (C, ·) is a cyclic group of order 4 generated by 3:
- 3¹ = 3, 3² = 9, 3³ = 7, 3⁴ = 1 = e.

**Proof.** (Z/10Z)* ≅ (Z/2Z)* × (Z/5Z)* ≅ {1} × Z/4Z ≅ Z/4Z by CRT. Direct multiplication mod 10 gives 3² = 9, 3³ = 7, 3⁴ = 1. Since |C| = 4 and ⟨3⟩ has order 4, C = ⟨3⟩. □

**Remark 2.3 (CREATION and DISSOLUTION orbits, corrected).** Under × 3 on Z/10Z, the unit set C is a single orbit of size 4:
- 1 → 3 → 9 → 7 → 1 (the *CREATION orbit*, visiting LATTICE–PROGRESS–RESET–HARMONY).

The complementary orbit on {2, 4, 6, 8} is also size 4:
- 2 → 6 → 8 → 4 → 2 (the *DISSOLUTION orbit*, visiting COUNTER–CHAOS–BREATH–COLLAPSE).

The × 3-fixed residues outside C are BALANCE = 5 and VOID = 0. Under × 2, the orbit structure on {2, 4, 6, 8} is recovered in the opposite direction (2 → 4 → 8 → 6 → 2); × 2 does *not* preserve C.

## §3 TSML closure of C

**Theorem 3.1 (TSML closure).** Let CL_TSML be the canonical TSML composition lattice on Z/10Z. Then C = {1, 3, 7, 9} is CL_TSML-closed. The 16 cells of CL_TSML ∣_{C × C} distribute as 14 cells of value 7 (HARMONY) and 2 cells of value 3 (PROGRESS); the two PROGRESS cells are at the symmetric pair (3, 9) and (9, 3).

**Proof.** Direct extraction from CL_TSML:

| CL_TSML | j=1 | j=3 | j=7 | j=9 |
|--------:|----:|----:|----:|----:|
| i=1 | 7 | 7 | 7 | 7 |
| i=3 | 7 | 7 | 7 | 3 |
| i=7 | 7 | 7 | 7 | 7 |
| i=9 | 7 | 3 | 7 | 7 |

Image = {3, 7} ⊆ C; 14 cells = 7, 2 cells = 3. The TSML table is symmetric, so RAW and SYM forms agree. □

**Corollary 3.2.** On C × C the HARMONY rate is 14/16 = 87.5%, higher than the global TSML HARMONY rate 73/100 = 73%; the remaining 2/16 = 12.5% are PROGRESS cells.

## §4 The corner C vs the joint-chain 4-core

### §4.1 C is not BHML-closed

**Proposition 4.1 (BHML non-closure of C).** The corner C = {1, 3, 7, 9} is *not* closed under the canonical BHML composition lattice CL_BHML. Every one of the 16 cells of CL_BHML ∣_{C × C} falls outside C.

**Proof.** Direct extraction:

| CL_BHML | j=1 | j=3 | j=7 | j=9 |
|--------:|----:|----:|----:|----:|
| i=1 | 2 | 4 | 2 | 6 |
| i=3 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 6 |
| i=7 | 2 | 4 | 8 | 0 |
| i=9 | 6 | 6 | 0 | 0 |

Image = {0, 2, 4, 6, 8}, disjoint from C = {1, 3, 7, 9}. Cell distribution: 3 cells of 0, 3 cells of 2, 5 cells of 4, 4 cells of 6, 1 cell of 8 (total 16). Every product of two units under CL_BHML lies in the non-unit set {0, 2, 4, 6, 8}. □

**Remark 4.2 (retraction of lens-invariance).** An earlier draft of this paper asserted that C was a lens-invariant sub-magma — closed under TSML, BHML, and a third lens STD simultaneously. Proposition 4.1 disproves the BHML half of that claim. The lens-invariance assertion is *formally retracted* in this revision. The honest scope is: C is TSML-closed (Theorem 3.1); C is not BHML-closed (Proposition 4.1); closure or non-closure of C under the third lens CL_STD is left to a future paper on the STD substrate.

### §4.2 Uniqueness of joint closure at size 4

**Theorem 4.3 (C vs the joint 4-core).** At size 4 in the substrate (Z/10Z, CL_TSML, CL_BHML):

1. There are exactly **78** subsets of Z/10Z of size 4 that are CL_TSML-closed (all of them contain HARMONY = 7).
2. There is exactly **one** subset of Z/10Z of size 4 that is CL_BHML-closed: the joint 4-core {0, 7, 8, 9}.
3. Therefore, there is exactly **one** subset of size 4 that is jointly CL_TSML- and CL_BHML-closed: the joint 4-core {0, 7, 8, 9}.

C = {1, 3, 7, 9} is one of the 78 TSML-closed 4-subsets. It is not the joint 4-core; it is not CL_BHML-closed. The intersection C ∩ {0, 7, 8, 9} = {7, 9} = {HARMONY, RESET}.

**Proof.** By exhaustive enumeration over the 210 size-4 subsets of Z/10Z applying the closure check (verified in `manuscript/verification/4core_verification.py`). All 78 TSML-closed 4-subsets contain HARMONY = 7 because HARMONY is the universal absorbing top of CL_TSML. □

**Remark (Structural Distinction).** Theorem 4.3 sharpens the contrast between C and the joint 4-core into a structural fact:

- C realizes the multiplicative-unit subgroup of Z/10Z as a TSML-closed sub-magma; it is one of 78 TSML-closed 4-subsets, distinguished only by its ring-theoretic content (C = (Z/10Z)* ≅ Z/4Z), not by closure properties alone.
- The joint 4-core {0, 7, 8, 9} is the *unique* 4-subset closed under both CL_TSML and CL_BHML; it is distinguished by joint closure alone, and its existence is the "unit cell" of the joint TSML+BHML chain (Sanders–Gish, J02/J35).

The two sub-magmas answer different structural questions about (Z/10Z, CL_TSML, CL_BHML): C asks "which TSML-closed subset of Z/10Z is also a ring-theoretic group?" (answer: C, uniquely); the joint 4-core asks "which subset is closed under all binary operations of the substrate?" (answer: {0, 7, 8, 9}, uniquely at size 4).

## §5 Generator selection: the unique g = 3

(Z/10Z)* ≅ Z/4Z has exactly two generators (order 4): g = 3 and g = 7. The canonical choice for the operator-name register is g = 3.

**Theorem 5.1 (Generator selection, D19).** Among the two primitive roots g ∈ {3, 7} of (Z/10Z)*, only g = 3 is compatible with the operator-name assignment
- LATTICE = g, PROGRESS = g², HARMONY = g³, RESET = g⁴ = 1,

together with BALANCE = 5 and the flatness ratio
- T* := BALANCE/HARMONY ∈ (0, 1).

Under g = 7, HARMONY = 7³ ≡ 3 (mod 10), so T* = 5/3 > 1, exiting the unit interval.

**Proof.** 3³ = 27 ≡ 7 (mod 10), so T* = 5/7 ∈ (0, 1). 7³ = 343 ≡ 3 (mod 10), so T* = 5/3 > 1. The elements of order 4 in (Z/10Z)* are exactly {3, 7}; g = 1 and g = 9 have order 1 and 2 respectively, so they do not generate C. □

**Remark 5.2.** The full TIG flatness theorem T* = 5/7 derives this ratio from several independent algebraic chains (see J07). The proof of Theorem 5.1 here uses only the ring-theoretic data (Z/10Z, g, g³) and the elementary inequality BALANCE < HARMONY.

## §6 Discussion

### §6.1 Position in the sub-magma inventory

The joint 4-core {0, 7, 8, 9} is the unique 4-element jointly closed sub-magma of Z/10Z under TSML+BHML. The corner C is one of 78 TSML-closed 4-subsets, distinguished by its ring-theoretic content. At larger sizes, the joint chain has shells at sizes {1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10} (J02/J35 Theorem 1); cataloguing the full TSML-only structure is left to future work.

### §6.2 Position in the small-magma literature

The closest published neighbor on small finite commutative non-associative magma structures is Drápal & Wanless, *J. Combin. Theory A* **184** (2021) 105510, which constructs maximally non-associative quasigroups (σ = 1) via quadratic orthomorphisms. The (Z/10Z, CL_TSML, CL_BHML) substrate sits at the *opposite* extremum: both tables exhibit substantial associativity (CL_TSML has 73 HARMONY cells, forming a large absorbing region) and the combinatorial content lies in the closure structure of small subsets (this paper) and in the joint-closure chain (J02/J35), not in non-associativity itself. The two directions share the domain (finite commutative non-associative algebraic structures of small order) but address opposite extrema.

### §6.3 What this paper does *not* establish

- *Lens-invariance of C.* C is TSML-closed only; it is not BHML-closed (Proposition 4.1). Closure of C under the third lens CL_STD is left open.
- *Generality.* Theorem 3.1 is a 16-cell direct verification on a specific table. We do not attempt a structural characterization of which subsets of Z/10Z are TSML-closed; the 78-element catalog is enumerated but not classified.
- *A general theorem on ring-theoretic subgroups vs sub-magmas of a given composition table on Z/n Z.* The natural follow-up (whether (Z/n Z)* is closed under a canonical TSML-analogue when one exists) is left to future work.
