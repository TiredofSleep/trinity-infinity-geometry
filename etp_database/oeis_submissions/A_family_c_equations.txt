%N The 14 equation IDs of Family C: the implication-closure of x*y = y*x (equation 43, commutativity) in the depth-3 enumeration of magma equations of Tao et al.'s Equational Theories Project (ETP).
%C This is a finite sequence of length 14.
%C In Tao et al.'s ETP (Tao, Anthony, Kim, et al. 2024-2025), the equation x*y = y*x is assigned the ID 43 under the canonical depth-≤3 enumeration of magma equations on the alphabet {x, y, z, ...}. The implication-closure of equation 43 — the set of all ETP equations forced to hold whenever commutativity holds — is exactly this 14-element set.
%C The 12 derived equations (4283 through 4677) are precisely the equations obtained by applying commutativity at a single position in an expression of depth ≤ 3. They include the cubic power-associativity (4380), several flexibility-like identities (4398, 4435), and the general "comm-in-outer" rule (4677).
%C This 14-element set is the smallest equational profile achievable by a commutative magma at orders ≥ 5 (the "commutativity-forced minimum"). Conjecturally — verified at orders 3 and 5 by exhaustive enumeration — it is the unique 14-element equational closure containing equation 43 over magmas with ≥ 5 elements.
%C Realizers include the σ-magma at order 10 (x ⋄ y = σ((x+y) mod 10) with σ = [0,7,1,3,2,4,5,6,8,9]) and 120 commutative non-associative magmas at order 3 (Trinity Infinity Geometry, J60, J61).
%C The closure is verified via ETP's 22-million-edge implication graph.
