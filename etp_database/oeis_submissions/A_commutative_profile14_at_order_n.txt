%S A_commutative_profile14_at_order_n 0,0,120,?,480
%N Number of commutative magmas of order n whose ETP equational profile equals Family C (the implication-closure of commutativity; see A_family_c_equations).
%C The sequence counts magmas T: [n]×[n]→[n] satisfying:
%C   (i) T[i][j] = T[j][i] (commutative)
%C   (ii) the set of ETP equations satisfied by T equals exactly the 14-element Family C closure
%C   (iii) NOT considering isomorphism (raw count of labeled magmas).
%C a(1) = a(2) = 0 (orders 1 and 2 don't support commutativity-forced minimum; degenerate cases).
%C a(3) = 120 (verified by exhaustive enumeration of all 729 commutative order-3 magmas; T-3 enumeration, Trinity Infinity Geometry 2026).
%C a(4) = unknown (order 4 enumeration pending).
%C a(5) = 480 (verified by enumeration of all 720 symmetric 5×5 Latin squares; all 480 profile-14 cases are in Family C).
%C This sequence verifies Conjecture 1 of J60 / J61: every commutative magma achieving the profile-14 floor is in Family C. (Theorem at n=3, 5.)

%E NOTE: This sequence is PARTIAL — only terms a(1), a(2), a(3), a(5) verified by exhaustive enumeration. a(4) and a(n) for n ≥ 6 require additional enumeration.
