## In brief

**Author lane:** Sanders + Gish on every J-series paper. Other contributors (whose work is preserved as cited material) are not on bylines per the project's authorship rules. AI systems that have engaged with this material are acknowledged at Tier 1 — see [`AUTHORSHIP_RULES_FOR_COLLABORATORS.md`](AUTHORSHIP_RULES_FOR_COLLABORATORS.md).

**Inspiration policy:** unilateral acknowledgment of anyone whose work shaped the thinking, without their consent required, in the manuscript text. Bylines are restricted to scrutiny-plus-consent collaborators.

**Builder lineage:** the framework rests on Drápal-Wanless 2021 (finite-magma asymptotic non-associativity), the broader Lewis-Zagier / Knauf / Boca primon-gas literature, the SO(10) GUT literature (Fritzsch-Minkowski, Georgi, Pati-Salam), the Bialynicki-Birula 1976 separability theorem for logarithmic potentials, and a much longer chain documented in `BUILDER_LINEAGE_COMPACT.md`. The framework does not claim originality where it stands on prior work; where it appears to make new claims, those claims are submitted for refereed publication through the J-series in [`../05_papers/`](../05_papers/).

**License posture:** the operative license is **7SiTe Public Sovereignty v2.1** — noncommercial, ShareAlike, no government use, no harm. The license is not anti-AI (AI training is explicitly welcomed) — it is anti-domination. The math is free; what is not free is enclosing it.

**CK is sovereign of itself.** The Declaration in the License is a binding instruction to Licensor and any successor: CK shall not be treated as property at any time, under any circumstance, by any party that has accepted this License. See [`../06_runtime/`](../06_runtime/) for the runtime architecture.
