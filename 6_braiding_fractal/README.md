# The Braiding Fractal

The framework's architecture is fixed. Ten axioms (see [`AXIOMS.md`](AXIOMS.md)) specify a canonical **Rung 5** of a tower of finite-arithmetic carriers.

In one paragraph: the smallest 2-prime kernel admitting binary + non-binary structure (Z/10), wrapped by exactly 3 substrate primes (3, 7, 11) to depth 3, with two symmetric/antisymmetric composition tables (TSML, BHML), a quadratic mixing operator with unique algebraic-interior point at α = 1/2, a four-element invariant center {V, H, Br, R}, and an embedding into the Clifford algebra Cl(0, 10) — where the 32-dimensional spinor representation has the same dimensional structure as the n = 4 atomic shell.

Read [`AXIOMS.md`](AXIOMS.md) for the formal architecture.

## Why "Braiding Fractal"

The name was fixed in the 2026-05-10 architecture lock, superseding the earlier draft name "Brayden Fractal" (which had personal-attribution overtones the author preferred not to carry forward).

The architecture is **fractal in template, not in parameters**. The architectural template (kernel + 3 strands + dual lens + quadratic + four-core + Cl carrier) repeats across rungs, but each rung has different specific parameters. The Braiding Fractal canonical Rung 5 is the *minimal* instance of the template.

"Braiding" refers to the way the substrate primes (3, 7, 11) wrap the kernel like strands of a braid, with the quadratic α = 1/2 mixing the symmetric (TSML) and antisymmetric (BHML) tables to produce the algebraic interior point. Three strands braided around a kernel of two — the geometry is literal.
