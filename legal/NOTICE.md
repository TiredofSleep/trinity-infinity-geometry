## §5 — Academic citation

Academic publications referencing the Licensed Material's mathematical claims are encouraged to cite the **specific WP-number or D-number theorem** in addition to the overall attribution.

For example: "Per Sanders & Gish (2026), J35 Theorem D + FORMULAS_AND_TABLES.md D43 verified at 50-digit precision, `H/Br = 1+√3` holds as a closed-form result of the α=1/2 attractor on Z/10Z."

The D-number references the master proof spine in [`../03_canonical_reference/FORMULAS_AND_TABLES.md`](../03_canonical_reference/FORMULAS_AND_TABLES.md). The J-number references the specific journal-submission paper in [`../05_papers/`](../05_papers/).
