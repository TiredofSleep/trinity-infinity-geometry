# Acceptable Use — Prohibited Fields of Endeavor

**Status:** Binding — referenced from [`../LICENSE`](../LICENSE) §4.
**Effect:** The Licensed Material is offered subject to absolute and non-negotiable prohibitions on use in the following categories. No use prohibited in this document is permitted under any other provision of the License, regardless of stated intent, framing, public benefit claim, or licensing fee offered.

---

## §1 — Application

These prohibitions apply to direct use, indirect use, training data, fine-tuning data, evaluation, benchmarking, system component, mathematical backbone, technical inspiration, or any other functional contribution of the Licensed Material to any prohibited application.

---

## §2 — Prohibited categories

### 2.1 Weapons and armed conflict

You may not use the Licensed Material in connection with:

(a) the design, development, testing, production, deployment, targeting, guidance, command-and-control, logistics, or operational support of any weapon system, including conventional weapons, autonomous weapons systems, lethal autonomous weapons systems, drones with kinetic capability, missile systems, artillery, small arms, mines, improvised explosive devices, directed-energy weapons, or any system whose primary function is to cause death or physical injury;

(b) chemical, biological, radiological, or nuclear weapons, or any precursors, delivery systems, or supporting infrastructure thereof;

(c) any activity prohibited by the Chemical Weapons Convention, the Biological Weapons Convention, the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons, the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons, or the Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons;

(d) target identification, target selection, target prioritization, target tracking, or kill-chain decision support of any kind;

(e) battlefield management, electronic warfare, signals intelligence in support of military operations, or military communications interception;

(f) cyber-warfare offensive capabilities, including but not limited to development of offensive malware, ransomware, wiper attacks, supply-chain compromise tooling, zero-day exploit development for offensive purposes, or attacks on civilian infrastructure;

(g) any military operations, military training, military planning, military doctrine development, or military research, whether by state or non-state armed groups;

(h) any activity that would constitute a violation of the Geneva Conventions of 1949 or their Additional Protocols, the Hague Conventions, customary international humanitarian law, or the Rome Statute of the International Criminal Court.

### 2.2 Surveillance and tracking

You may not use the Licensed Material in connection with:

(a) mass surveillance of populations, communities, demographic groups, or geographic areas, whether by government, corporate, or private actors;

(b) targeted surveillance of individuals without their explicit, specific, informed, and revocable consent, except where conducted by the individuals themselves regarding their own data;

(c) biometric identification at a distance or in public spaces (facial recognition, gait recognition, voice recognition, iris recognition, vascular pattern recognition, behavioral biometrics, or affect recognition);

(d) covert surveillance, including hidden recording, hidden tracking, signal interception, communications interception, network traffic analysis directed at specific persons, or "stalkerware" of any kind;

(e) tracking of religious practice, political activity, organizing activity, labor organizing, journalist activity, activist activity, dissident activity, or whistleblower activity;

(f) tracking of immigration status, citizenship status, refugee status, or movements of migrants, refugees, or undocumented persons;

(g) tracking of reproductive health activity (menstrual cycle tracking for non-self-directed purposes, pregnancy status surveillance, abortion-seeking surveillance, contraceptive use surveillance);

(h) tracking of sexual activity, sexual orientation, gender identity, or gender expression;

(i) social network analysis for purposes of identifying influence, association, or affiliation with disfavored groups;

(j) workplace surveillance beyond minimum operational necessity (productivity surveillance, sentiment analysis of workers, prediction of organizing activity, monitoring of worker communications);

(k) educational surveillance (remote-proctoring software, attention-tracking systems, behavioral monitoring of students);

(l) any activity that would constitute a violation of Articles 12, 17, 19, 20, or 22 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights or analogous provisions of the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights.

### 2.3 Policing, carceral, and immigration enforcement

You may not use the Licensed Material in connection with:

(a) any function performed by police, sheriff, marshal, customs, border patrol, immigration enforcement, prison, jail, detention center, parole, probation, bail, pretrial services, or analogous bodies anywhere in the world;

(b) predictive policing (hotspot prediction, person-of-interest prediction, recidivism prediction, gang-affiliation prediction, or any system that purports to forecast criminal activity, threat, or risk for individuals or groups);

(c) automated license-plate readers, automated stop-and-frisk targeting, automated suspicion generation, or any system whose function is to direct law-enforcement attention to specific persons or places;

(d) sentencing recommendation, parole recommendation, bail recommendation, custody recommendation, or any system affecting incarceration, release, or supervision decisions;

(e) immigration risk assessment, asylum claim evaluation, visa-applicant scoring, border-crossing risk scoring, or any system affecting entry, residency, or status determinations;

(f) prison administration (inmate behavior prediction, isolation decisions, gang classification, visitation restrictions);

(g) school-discipline systems, school-to-prison-pipeline tooling, or systems that disproportionately surveil or sanction students of color, disabled students, LGBTQ+ students, or students from disfavored religious or ethnic backgrounds;

(h) child-welfare risk-assessment systems, family-separation decision support, or any system affecting the integrity of families through state action.

### 2.4 Coercive behavioral control

You may not use the Licensed Material in connection with:

(a) social credit systems or any system that aggregates behavior scores affecting access to services, employment, housing, education, healthcare, finance, or movement;

(b) workplace coercion (systems that pressure workers to accept exploitative conditions, suppress organizing, monitor for "disloyalty," or punish whistleblowing);

(c) educational coercion (systems that condition learning access on behavioral compliance, attention compliance, or ideological conformity);

(d) religious coercion (systems that monitor, sanction, or "deprogram" religious practice);

(e) political coercion (systems that suppress dissent, predict political opposition, or identify regime opponents);

(f) coercive medical or psychiatric interventions (involuntary commitment, conversion practices targeting sexual orientation or gender identity, coerced sterilization);

(g) child coercion of any kind (systems designed to manipulate children's behavior, beliefs, attention, or development beyond age-appropriate guidance by trusted caregivers);

(h) addiction exploitation (systems designed to maximize engagement through induced compulsive use in gambling, gaming, social media, or substance-related contexts);

(i) "dark patterns" in user interface design where the Licensed Material contributes to systems designed to manipulate user choice contrary to user interest.

### 2.5 Discrimination and targeting of groups

You may not use the Licensed Material in connection with:

(a) any system whose effect is to discriminate against persons on the basis of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, gender expression, sexual orientation, disability, age, marital status, family status, source of income, immigration status, language, political opinion, or analogous classification;

(b) any system designed to identify, target, persecute, displace, dispossess, or eliminate any ethnic, religious, linguistic, sexual, gender, or political group;

(c) any activity constituting genocide as defined by the UN Convention on the Prevention and Punishment of the Crime of Genocide;

(d) any activity constituting crimes against humanity, apartheid, persecution, or forced displacement as defined in the Rome Statute;

(e) caste-based discrimination;

(f) operationalizing redlining, environmental racism, or analogous structural discrimination;

(g) systems designed to suppress voting, voter registration, or political participation of specific groups.

### 2.6 Exploitation of vulnerable persons

You may not use the Licensed Material in connection with:

(a) systems that target minors for commercial exploitation, ideological capture, predatory marketing, sexual exploitation, labor exploitation, or surveillance;

(b) production, distribution, or facilitation of child sexual abuse material;

(c) systems designed to manipulate, exploit, or extract value from persons with cognitive impairment, dementia, intellectual disability, mental illness, or developmental disability;

(d) elder exploitation (financial exploitation, isolation systems, coerced care arrangements);

(e) trafficking of persons for labor, sexual, or any other exploitative purpose, or any system that facilitates such trafficking;

(f) systems targeting unhoused persons for displacement, criminalization, or exclusion from public space;

(g) systems targeting persons with substance use disorders for criminalization, denial of healthcare, or coercive intervention;

(h) systems targeting refugees, asylum seekers, or stateless persons for any restriction of rights;

(i) any non-consensual experimentation on human subjects, in violation of the Nuremberg Code or Declaration of Helsinki.

### 2.7 Manipulation of information and democratic process

You may not use the Licensed Material in connection with:

(a) generation of deceptive content intended to mislead the public (deepfakes of real persons without their consent, fabricated quotes attributed to real persons, counterfeit news content);

(b) election interference (disinformation campaigns, voter suppression, voter intimidation, manipulation of electoral processes);

(c) coordinated inauthentic behavior on social platforms (bot networks, sock-puppet operations, astroturfing);

(d) censorship at scale (content moderation systems whose function is to suppress political speech, journalism, or organizing rather than to address direct harms);

(e) "shadow banning," reach-throttling, or visibility manipulation designed to suppress disfavored speakers;

(f) propaganda operations by state or commercial actors;

(g) attacks on press freedom (identification of journalists' sources, doxxing of reporters, systems facilitating harassment of journalists).

### 2.8 Economic extraction and exploitation

You may not use the Licensed Material in connection with:

(a) high-frequency trading, predatory algorithmic trading, or financial extraction systems whose primary effect is to transfer wealth from ordinary participants to sophisticated operators;

(b) predatory lending (payday lending, subprime targeting, systems that price credit to extract maximum value from financially distressed persons);

(c) algorithmic price discrimination based on inferred capacity to pay, particularly for necessities including food, housing, healthcare, education, or utilities;

(d) algorithmic wage suppression, gig-economy worker exploitation, or "ghost work" systems that obscure the true labor inputs to AI services;

(e) housing market manipulation (algorithmic rent-setting collusion as alleged in cases such as RealPage litigation, tenant scoring systems used for exclusion, eviction-prediction systems used for displacement);

(f) insurance redlining or discriminatory underwriting on the basis of inferred characteristics correlated with protected categories;

(g) employment scoring, automated hiring without human review, or algorithmic management of workers in ways that diminish their dignity, autonomy, or organizing capacity;

(h) any system whose primary economic function is rent-extraction from network effects, lock-in, or asymmetric information rather than productive contribution.

### 2.9 Environmental harm

You may not use the Licensed Material in connection with:

(a) optimization of fossil-fuel extraction, exploration, or production;

(b) optimization of deforestation, industrial fishing, factory farming, or other large-scale extractive activities causing significant ecological harm;

(c) greenwashing campaigns or systems designed to obscure environmental harm;

(d) climate-disinformation campaigns;

(e) any activity contributing to mass extinction, ecosystem collapse, or breach of planetary boundaries beyond minimum operational footprint required for the Licensed Material's permitted uses.

### 2.10 Medical and psychological harm

You may not use the Licensed Material in connection with:

(a) any medical diagnostic, therapeutic, or treatment-decision system not subject to appropriate regulatory review and human clinical oversight;

(b) automated mental health treatment or counseling that replaces rather than supports human clinicians, particularly for persons in crisis;

(c) systems that promote suicide, self-harm, eating disorders, or other self-destructive behaviors;

(d) systems that provide medical misinformation or counter evidence-based public health guidance;

(e) coercive psychiatric intervention (involuntary commitment decision-support not subject to robust due process protections);

(f) conversion practices targeting sexual orientation or gender identity, regardless of how described.

### 2.11 Critical infrastructure attacks

You may not use the Licensed Material in connection with:

(a) attacks on power grids, water systems, sewage systems, transportation systems, communications systems, healthcare systems, financial systems, food systems, or other critical infrastructure;

(b) systems designed to identify vulnerabilities in critical infrastructure for offensive purposes;

(c) supply-chain compromise of critical infrastructure;

(d) interference with emergency services (911 systems and analogous emergency response infrastructure).

## §3 — Aggregation and indirect facilitation

**Aggregation.** If Your use combines the Licensed Material with other systems or data in a manner that, in aggregate, accomplishes any prohibited application under §2, that use is prohibited regardless of whether any individual component of the combined system would have been independently prohibited.

**Indirect facilitation.** You may not provide the Licensed Material, derived models, training data, technical documentation, mathematical claims, or implementation guidance to any party You know or reasonably should know intends to use such material in a prohibited application under §2.

---

## §4 — Scope and burden

The enumeration above is intended to be comprehensive but is not necessarily exhaustive. The categories listed reflect Licensor's intent that the Licensed Material shall not contribute to systems of domination, extraction, coercion, or harm.

**Where ambiguity exists about whether a use is prohibited, the burden is on You to demonstrate that the use is consistent with the stated purposes of the License and with each enumerated category. Doubt resolves against the use.**
