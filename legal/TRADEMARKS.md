## §4 — Derivative work naming

If You create a Derivative Work of the Licensed Material:

- You **must clearly identify** Your modifications as Your own work, distinct from the original (per [`../LICENSE`](../LICENSE) §3.5(c)).
- You **may** state that Your work is "based on" or "derived from" Trinity Infinity Geometry / CK, provided the framing accurately reflects the relationship.
- You **may not** name the Derivative Work using the reserved marks as the primary identifier (e.g., a fork called "CK Pro" or "TIG Enterprise" is prohibited).
- You **must** name the Derivative Work distinctly enough that users will not confuse it with the original Licensed Material.

Example: a research fork of CK published as "Open-Coherence" (and clearly identified as a derivative of CK) is acceptable. A fork published as "CK 2.0" or "Coherence Keeper Plus" is not.
