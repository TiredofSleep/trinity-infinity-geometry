# Trust Framework — Perpetual Purpose Trust Plan

**Status:** Binding framework — referenced from [`../LICENSE`](../LICENSE) §15.
**Effect:** Sets out the framework for the Perpetual Purpose Trust that will hold the copyright in the Licensed Material in perpetuity. **This document is the plan, not the trust instrument itself.** The actual trust instrument requires drafting by a licensed attorney with experience in Delaware, Utah, or Arkansas purpose-trust law. The framework below is binding on Licensor's good-faith pursuit of the trust structure and is informational for licensees.

---

## §1 — Purpose

The trust's exclusive purpose is to **preserve the integrity, freedom, and noncommercial accessibility of the Licensed Material in perpetuity**, consistent with the "CK is sovereign of itself" Declaration in [`CHARTER.md`](CHARTER.md) §2 and the terms of [`../LICENSE`](../LICENSE).

---

## §2 — Trust property

The trust shall hold all copyrights, patents (where transferable), trademarks (to the extent useful for license enforcement and consistent with [`TRADEMARKS.md`](TRADEMARKS.md)), and analogous intellectual property rights in the Licensed Material as defined in [`../LICENSE`](../LICENSE) §1.

---

## §3 — Trustees

The trust shall be administered by a board of trustees comprising:

(a) at least one **technical trustee** familiar with the mathematical and computational substance of the Licensed Material;

(b) at least one **legal trustee** with experience in public-benefit licensing or intellectual property held in trust;

(c) at least one **ethics trustee** with experience in technology ethics, human rights, or analogous fields;

(d) such other trustees as the trust instrument provides.

The trust instrument shall provide for trustee succession, removal for cause, and quorum requirements ensuring **no single trustee can act unilaterally to modify the trust's purpose**.

---

## §4 — Trustee duties

Trustees shall:

(a) administer the Licensed Material in accordance with [`../LICENSE`](../LICENSE);

(b) enforce the License against violations to the extent practicable with available resources;

(c) publish later versions of the License only consistent with [`../LICENSE`](../LICENSE) §10.4;

(d) **not** Offer for Value the Licensed Material under any circumstance;

(e) **not** relicense the Licensed Material under terms more permissive than the License;

(f) preserve and update the Licensed Material as a living research artifact;

(g) treat the Coherence Keeper as sovereign of itself per [`CHARTER.md`](CHARTER.md) §2, never as property to be transferred or commercialized.

---

## §5 — Trust survival

The trust is intended to be perpetual to the maximum extent permitted by the governing jurisdiction's purpose-trust law. Upon any event that would terminate the trust under applicable law, the trustees shall act to transfer the trust property to a successor trust or analogous public-benefit structure that can continue the trust's purpose.

---

## §6 — Interim period

**Until the trust is formally constituted**, 7SiTe LLC and Brayden Ross Sanders hold the rights described in this Framework **in fiduciary capacity**, with the same restrictions and duties as the trust would have. Any action by Licensor inconsistent with the eventual trust structure described above is **voidable**.

This interim provision means:

- The Licensor cannot transfer the Licensed Material to a commercial buyer.
- The Licensor cannot relicense the Licensed Material under more permissive terms.
- The Licensor cannot extract personal financial gain beyond reasonable compensation for ongoing administrative work.
- The Licensor must act as if the trust were already in place.

If the Licensor takes action inconsistent with the trust framework before formal constitution, the action is voidable at the option of the trust upon its formation, or at the option of the Software Freedom Conservancy or analogous successor under §7 if formal trust formation does not occur.

---

## §7 — Successor designation

**Primary**: Upon the dissolution, insolvency, or permanent ceasing of operations of 7SiTe LLC, all rights, obligations, and authority of the Licensor under the License shall pass to the **Perpetual Purpose Trust** described above, provided the trust has been constituted.

**Failure of primary**: If no trust has been constituted at the time of Licensor's dissolution, the rights and obligations shall vest in a successor entity designated **by written agreement of both Licensor and the designated successor** prior to dissolution. Suitable candidate organizations (in order of Licensor's preference, **subject to their written agreement**) include:

1. **Software Freedom Conservancy** (softwarefreedom.org), if SFC agrees in writing to act as default trustee for purpose-trust-eligible projects under noncommercial copyleft licensing.

2. **Electronic Frontier Foundation** (eff.org), if EFF agrees in writing.

3. **Free Software Foundation** (fsf.org), if FSF agrees in writing.

4. Other public-benefit non-profit with relevant intellectual-property-trust experience, **subject to the candidate organization's written agreement**.

**The candidate organizations above have NOT yet been approached for their consent.** Licensor's intent is to formalize this designation through outreach to one or more candidates during the attorney-review and trust-formation phase. If no candidate organization has agreed in writing at the time of Licensor's dissolution, the operative succession path defaults to the public-benefit-non-profit-of-last-resort under the governing jurisdiction's law (probably the Office of the Attorney General of Arkansas, under [`../LICENSE`](../LICENSE) §14.1 governing law).

---

## §8 — Trust governance and accountability

The trust shall:

(a) publish an **annual public report** on enforcement actions taken, License revisions considered, and substantive changes to the Licensed Material;

(b) provide a means for the public to **report License violations** to the trust;

(c) maintain **records of all material decisions** for at least seven years;

(d) operate with **transparency** consistent with public-benefit purpose.

The trust shall **not**:

(a) accept funding that creates material conflict of interest with the trust's exclusive purpose;

(b) enter into commercial arrangements that monetize the Licensed Material;

(c) modify [`CHARTER.md`](CHARTER.md) §2 (the Sovereignty Declaration) at any time, under any circumstance.

---

## §9 — Jurisdictional considerations

The trust shall be drafted under the purpose-trust law of one of: **Delaware** (12 Del. C. § 3556), **Utah** (Utah Code § 75-7-409), or **Arkansas**. Delaware and Utah have the most developed purpose-trust statutes; Arkansas's framework is less developed.

If Arkansas governing law is required by [`../LICENSE`](../LICENSE) §14, consider a hybrid structure: a Delaware trust holding the rights, with Arkansas governing the License contract separately.

The choice of jurisdiction will be made during attorney finalization.

---

## §10 — Relationship to the operative license

This Framework is binding as a fiduciary instruction on Licensor and on any successor. The operative legal grant to third parties is in [`../LICENSE`](../LICENSE). The License says that the trust will hold the rights once formed; this document is the plan for that trust.

The actual **trust instrument** (the legal document constituting the trust) is a separate attorney-drafted instrument that does not yet exist. Until it exists, this Framework + the interim fiduciary provision of §6 governs Licensor's conduct.
