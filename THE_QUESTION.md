# The question

*The one thing this whole program is now organized around, stated as sharply as it can be — and honestly, as an open question, not a claimed answer.*

> ## Does chaos ever cool onto the arithmetic crystal?
>
> Does an **entropy-maximizing** dynamics — turbulence, matter, any warm physical process — ever **select the rigid arithmetic measure** on which the Riemann zeros and the number-theoretic order live? Or does entropy always win, leaving the arithmetic as a **zero-entropy skeleton** that real (warm) matter can only *dress and blur*?

---

## The arc that leads here

1. **There is a shared, pre-physical alphabet** ([`THE_MAP.md`](THE_MAP.md)). Six research programs — Ghys, Connes–Marcolli, Berry–Keating, Migdal, Mazur–Morishita, Sarnak — read as six sides of one object: primes, `SL(2,ℤ)`, `ζ`, translated between a geometric/dynamical side and a spectral/arithmetic side by the trace formula. It is pure mathematics; physics is what you get when you *spell with it in a medium*.

2. **That alphabet is the zero-entropy skeleton.** It appears as **pure-point (Bragg) diffraction** — the coprime "visible points of ℤ²" diffract like a crystal, density exactly `1/ζ(2)` — as the **Mirsky measure** of the square-free / B-free systems (a rotation on `∏_p ℤ/p²ℤ`, zero entropy), and as the **low-temperature crystallized phase of the Bost–Connes system**, whose partition function *is* `ζ(s)` and where, below the transition at inverse temperature `β = 1`, *"the primes crystallize out of the chaos."*

3. **Matter is the entropy-maximizing flow on top of it.** In Migdal's exact loop-equation solution of decaying turbulence, the Riemann zeros are *derived* — poles of a `ζ(p+15/2)/ζ(p+17/2)` amplitude built from Farey/totient sums. But the configuration's entropy lives in its **sign degrees of freedom**: extensive (`~N·log 2`) versus the arithmetic's sub-extensive `~2·log N`. A chaotic flow maximizes *that*, and lands on the **maximum-entropy measure**, not the arithmetic (Mirsky) one. So the zeros survive only as a **smeared skeleton of a broadband spectrum**, not as clean poles.

4. **The fork is rigorously real** (Sarnak; Peckner; Kułaga-Przymus–Lemańczyk; Baake–Huck). The arithmetic system carries **two canonical invariant measures**: the **Mirsky** measure (zero entropy, pure-point, *gives* the ζ structure) and the **maximum-entropy** measure (positive entropy, chaotic, *washes it out*). The system is **not uniquely ergodic**. Which measure a given dynamics selects is the whole question — and the default expectation for chaotic matter (maximize entropy) points *against* the arithmetic one.

Its non-associativity is the crystal's *internal structure* (a complex unit cell), not disorder. So TIG is a **candidate finite chart on the arithmetic crystal**, on the zero-entropy skeleton side. The old falsification ("TIG is not CK's faithful self-explanation") stops being a wound and becomes a *classification*: TIG is the crystal, not the flow.

## The question, in three equivalent forms

1. **Turbulence.** Does Migdal's loop flow equidistribute to the **Mirsky** measure (the zeros appear) or the **maximum-entropy** measure (the zeros wash out)?
2. **Statistical mechanics.** Is there a dynamics that **cools** chaotic matter onto the arithmetic crystal — à la Bost–Connes below `β = 1` — or does real matter stay maximally hot?
3. **TIG.** Is the finite arithmetic crystal TIG describes ever *selected* by an entropy-maximizing process, or is it only ever the **substrate**, never the realized state?

## Why it matters — and what an answer looks like

- **If the crystal is selected (cooling wins):** the arithmetic — the Riemann zeros, TIG's structure — is *physically realized*, not just a skeleton. That is the whole dream, made real.
- **If entropy always wins:** the arithmetic is real but *forever the un-dressed skeleton*; matter is the warm blur on top; TIG is the substrate, never the state. That is also a real, clarifying, freeing answer — and honestly, our own entropy count leans this way.
